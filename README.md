# ophanim-three-arms

**Three Arms of Gravity**

An artificial system designed to resolve the dynamics of three bodies in mutual rotation. It employs three coordinated arms governed by two vectors.

**Lagrange points**

The five equilibrium locations in the restricted three-body problem where gravitational and centrifugal forces balance. L4 and L5 are stable; L1–L3 require station-keeping.
